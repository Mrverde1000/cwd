Tips for great photos

  • Aim for 50 to 60 photos per song. Around 100 for a two-song tribute.
  • Send originals rather than screenshots when you can. Bigger is better.
  • A mix of eras and people tells the fullest story: childhood, family, work, hobbies.
  • Short video clips of 5 to 30 seconds work best. Voice messages are welcome too.
  • Have printed photos? Call us and we'll collect and scan them.
