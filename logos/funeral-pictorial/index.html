01 · Dove & Frame

Classic horizontal lockup. Dove of peace inside a photo frame.

02 · Candle Filmstrip

Square dark mark. A candle whose body is a strip of film.

03 · Infinity Film

Filmstrip looping into an infinity symbol with a gold heart.

04 · Lily Monogram

FP initials with a calla lily. Formal, stationery friendly.

05 · Sunset Horizon

Circular photo of a sunset over water in a frame.

06 · Open Album

Photo album with light and a heart rising from the pages.

07 · Wordmark

Typography only. The dot of the i is a play button.

08 · Lantern

A lantern holding photographs instead of a flame.

09 · Heart Frame

Tilted photo card with corner mounts and a soft heart.

10 · Willow Badge

Round seal with a weeping willow and curved text.
