# Funeral Pictorial — logos built on common funeral-home symbols

A second set of ten logos, each built around a symbol that funeral-home logo
guides and memorial-symbol references cite most often. Open `index.html` to see
them side by side, or look at `preview.png`.

## What the research showed

Design-industry roundups and memorial-symbol guides keep naming the same images.
Ranked by how often they came up:

1. **Dove** — peace, the soul, comfort. Named as the go-to symbol by nearly every source, which also makes it the hardest to own.
2. **Tree** — tree of life, oak, and especially the weeping willow, "a universal emblem of grief and mourning."
3. **Cross** — the most common memorial symbol in the Western world, used by faith-based homes.
4. **Candle / eternal flame** — "one of the most universal mourning symbols."
5. **Flowers** — calla lily and rose most often.
6. **Sunrise or sunset** — resurrection, a new day, the close of life.
7. **Clasped or cupped hands** — care and farewell.
8. **Butterfly** — transformation.
9. **Hearts and infinity loops** — abstract shapes for love and eternity.

Style guidance from the same sources: serif type reads as traditional and
personal; palettes lean navy, blue, green, grey and gold; yellow and orange are
avoided as too celebratory; the best logos use one or two colours and very
little imagery.
