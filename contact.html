What to send us

  • Photos from phones, computers or scanned prints, in any order
  • Short video clips or voice recordings, if you have them
  • Full name, dates, and any words or verses you'd like included
  • Favourite songs or hymns, or let us suggest gentle music
  • The venue name, so we can confirm their playback setup

Don't have everything yet? That's fine. Send what you have and we'll start.

What happens next

  1. We call you back within the hour

    To listen, confirm the date and send you a private upload link.

  2. You send the memories

    Upload, text, email or hand us the prints. We'll collect if needed.

  3. Preview within 24 hours

    Watch the first version from your phone and tell us any changes.

  4. Delivered and set up

    Files go to the funeral home, and we bring the TV if required.
