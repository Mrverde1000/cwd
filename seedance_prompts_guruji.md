# Seedance 2.5 prompts: "Guruji" — the Yangmunus concepts, retold by an elderly Indian teacher in Hindi

Built from two sources:

- **Yangmunus Videos – Concepts and Book Connections** (the 421-reel catalog and its mapping to Sivananda, Nadkarni's *Handbook of Hinduism*, Jain's *Hinduism and Jainism*, and the World Religions deck). Each prompt below names the theme it comes from and the Hindu/Ayurvedic term the book-connections section matched it to.
- **Seedance prompts guide** (the OpenArt / ByteDance rules). Every prompt follows them: labelled references with "only", whole-second timestamps with no gaps, one camera move per beat, sound in brackets, language named before each voice line, no captions.

The Chinese-medicine framing of the originals has been swapped for its Indian counterpart, as the book-connections document suggests: Qi → prana, "digestive fire of the spleen" → agni, monastery → ashram, "my teacher Dr. Lee who lived to 102" → "my Guruji who lived to 102 in the Himalayan foothills", foot soaks → padabhyanga (warm sesame-oil foot massage), Chinese tonics → haldi doodh and tulsi-adrak kadha, "monks eat one meal a day" → "our elders ate before sunset, Jain sadhus still do". The "pharmacies hate this" / "doctors lie" angle from the originals is left out; the health lines are kept at the level of traditional practice rather than cure claims.

## 1. How to use these

1. **Make the character once.** Run Prompt 0 (text to video, no references). Pick the best frame, save it to the OpenArt Characters library as "Guruji". Also save one clean frame of the kurta and mala, one of the courtyard, one of the kitchen, one of the ghat.
2. **From then on, show, don't describe.** Every prompt starts with the reference block below. Replace the image numbers with your own upload order. If you record a Hindi voice sample, add it as @Audio 1 so the voice stays the same across clips.
3. **Keep the speech in one language.** All voice lines are Hindi, written in Devanagari inside { } braces, with "Spoken language: Hindi" named before them (the guide's documented pattern). The English meaning is given under each prompt for you, not for the model. Do not paste the English into the prompt.
4. **Seedance 2.0 users:** replace "0-8s:", "8-18s:" with "Shot 1:", "Shot 2:" and so on. Everything else carries over.
5. **Longer videos:** generate 30 s, then use the extension prompt in section 5. Do not stitch.

## 2. Prompt 0: create the character (no references)

Mode: Text to Video. Use this once to produce the reference frames.

```
An Indian man of about eighty sits cross-legged on a thin cotton mat in a whitewashed ashram courtyard at dawn. Deep brown, weathered skin, a long white beard, white hair tied in a small knot at the back, warm dark eyes, a lean upright frame. He wears a plain white cotton kurta and dhoti, a saffron shawl over one shoulder, a rudraksha mala around his neck, and a small sandalwood tilak on his forehead. Beside him a brass lota of water and a small oil lamp; behind him a tulsi plant on a stone pedestal and a peepal tree.

0-10s: Wide static shot of the courtyard as soft dawn light comes over the wall. He sits with eyes closed, hands resting on his knees.
10-20s: Slow push in to a medium shot. He opens his eyes, breathes out slowly, and reaches for the lota.
20-30s: Static close-up on his face as he looks into the lens and gives a small, kind nod.

Spoken language: Hindi. He speaks slowly and warmly in a low, slightly gravelly voice, with natural pauses.
20-30s: {नमस्ते। बैठो। आज एक छोटी बात बताता हूँ।}
<birds at dawn> <a distant temple bell> （no background music, keep the courtyard sound）
No captions. No logos or watermarks.
```
Meaning: "Namaste. Sit. Today I'll tell you one small thing."
