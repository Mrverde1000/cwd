Frequently asked questions

Answers for a difficult week.

Common questions from families and funeral directors about our memorial slideshow service.

How quickly can you produce a memorial slideshow?

We understand how time-sensitive a service is. We deliver as soon as we can and always before the service date, typically within 24 to 48 hours of receiving the photos. If the timeline is tight, tell us when you order. Same-day rush service is available seven days a week.

How do we send you the family's photos?

Families upload photos, video clips and PDF obituaries or programs directly through our private online portal, from a phone or computer, with no account to create. Funeral home staff can also upload on a family's behalf from the home's own account. Printed photographs can be dropped off or collected for scanning.

What formats do you deliver the final video in?

We deliver digitally: a high-resolution MP4 file ready to play at the service, a keepsake USB drive, and a password-protected online link for family members who couldn't attend. We don't produce discs. If a family has old DVDs they'd like included, we can have them converted to digital files first.

Do we need any special equipment to play the video at the service?

No. The video plays on any computer, TV or projection system, and we deliver in universally compatible formats. If the venue has no screen at all, our Legacy + TV Setup package brings a television and speaker, sets them up, tests the slideshow and collects everything afterwards.

Can the family make changes after seeing the first version?

Yes. You receive a private preview link, and revisions are unlimited and included. Send as many notes as the family likes, and we'll build the next version, usually within a couple of hours.

How does the partnership work for funeral homes?

It's simple. You offer the tribute slideshow to the family, your staff places the order in your online account, and we handle the rest. Many funeral homes give it to the family as a gift or include it in their service packages. A short training video walks your staff through the portal.

Is there a cost to the funeral home to partner with us?

There is no fee or commitment to partner with us: no minimum, no monthly fee and no lock-in. You pay only for the slideshows you order, on one simple monthly invoice you can pay online.

Do you serve funeral homes outside the Chicago area?

Yes. Because the slideshow is handled digitally through our portal, we work with families and funeral homes anywhere in the United States. On-site TV delivery and setup is available across Chicagoland and the surrounding suburbs.

What if the family has very few photos?

We can create a beautiful tribute even with a limited number of photos. Gentle motion effects, meaningful quotes and well-chosen music let us build a touching video regardless of how many images are available.

How long do you keep a family's photos and materials?

The finished video is yours to keep, delivered as a download and on USB. We hold the original photos and materials as a courtesy for as long as we reasonably can, and delete them on request. If a file is ever lost, it's worth asking, because we can often still help. We always encourage families to keep their own copies.
