Questions

Answers for a difficult week.

How quickly can you have the slideshow ready?

Our standard turnaround is 24 to 48 hours from the moment we receive your photos. If the service is tomorrow, call us. We offer same-day rush service seven days a week and will do everything possible to be ready in time.

How do I send you the photos and videos?

Any way that's easy for you. Upload through the private link we send, text or email them, share a Google Photos or iCloud album, or drop off a box of prints and we'll scan them. We can also collect prints from your home or the funeral home.

Do you provide the TV if the venue doesn't have one?

Yes. Our Legacy + TV Setup package includes a 55-inch HD television on a stand with a speaker. We deliver it, set it up, test the slideshow and collect everything after the service. Additional screens are available for larger rooms.

Can we choose the music?

Absolutely. Tell us their favourite songs or hymns, or pick from our licensed library of gentle instrumentals. We'll time the slideshow so the music begins and ends naturally.

What if we want changes after seeing the first version?

Revisions are unlimited and included. You'll receive a private preview link. Tell us what to swap, reorder or remove and we'll send an updated version, usually within a couple of hours.

Will the file play on the funeral home's equipment?

Yes. We deliver a standard HD MP4 that plays on any TV, laptop or projector, plus a USB drive and online link. If you tell us the venue, we'll format it for their exact system and confirm it with them directly.
